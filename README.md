# Blog Wall
<div align="center">

Blog it is a blogging app where readers can find lots of insightful and informational articles related to a wide variety of topics that include technology, science, health, etc. Anyone can share their ideas through publishing their useful blogs and engage in conversation with a network of readers and writers by interacting with the content.




## 🔥 Features
📝 Enables to share your thoughts with other users through blogs and comments.

✨ Blogs Available in different categories depending upon the choice of Users.

🚀 Based on MVVM Architecture.

😍 Built Using latest Material UI design components.

📱 Simple email Login to keep the Users Authenticated.

## 🛠 Tech Used (Android)
- Android
- Kotlin
- Retrofit
- Live Data 
- Coroutines
- Glide
- Shimmer
- Pagination
- MVVM
